PROJECT 01 · 13–27 NOVEMBER 2026

Welcome to France in 3 sessions.

Can we help a new visitor find their way around a town in France using English?

Trois vendredis pour préparer, répéter et présenter une visite guidée en anglais.

📅 13 · 20 · 27 November⏱ 2 hours per session🤝 Work in pairs
🗺️ YOUR FINAL CHALLENGE A 3-stop guided tour

Introduce three places in a French town centre. Tell your visitor how to get there and what to do.

🎤 Aim for 45–60 seconds · Maximum 3 minutes
THE MISSION

Be a friendly local guide.

Imagine an English-speaking visitor is new to your town. Make them feel welcome and show them at least three interesting places.

Ton défi : faire découvrir trois lieux à un visiteur anglophone et l'aider à s'orienter.

Your success checklist
  1. Welcome your visitor politely.
  2. Introduce yourself.
  3. Present at least three places in town.
  4. Say what people can do in each place.
  5. Explain how to get there.
  6. Give one recommendation.
  7. Make sure both partners speak.

01 FRIDAY · 13 NOVEMBER · 120 MINUTESDiscover & planPlaces in town · choose your route ⌄

Today's goal Understand the visitor’s mission, recognise places in town, and choose three stops with your partner.

  1. 0–10 min
    Project briefing

    Discover the visitor scenario and your final mission.

  2. 10–28 min
    Watch and explore the video

    Notice what makes a guided tour helpful and clear.

  3. 28–43 min
    How do we plan a guided tour?

    Explore a model and learn the planning steps.

  4. 43–63 min
    Places in Town

    Learn and practise the names of common places.

  5. 63–73 min
    Break

    10-minute break.

  6. 73–91 min
    Where can I go?

    Match everyday needs to places in town.

  7. 91–110 min
    Choose your partner and places

    Select three stops and explain your choices.

  8. 110–120 min
    Planning card & exit ticket

    Write your first plan and identify your next step.

✓By the end: A first route plan with three places and a completed planning card.

02 FRIDAY · 20 NOVEMBER · 120 MINUTESFind the wayDirections · recommendations · rehearsal ⌄

Today's goal Give and follow directions, explain what visitors can do, and practise your tour together.

  1. 0–10 min
    Vocabulary retrieval

    Recall places in town through a short review game.

  2. 10–25 min
    Learn directions

    Practise simple phrases for finding your way.

  3. 25–45 min
    Map navigation exercises

    Follow and give directions with A1–A2 map tasks.

  4. 45–55 min
    Human GPS

    Guide a partner using spoken directions.

  5. 55–65 min
    Break

    10-minute break.

  6. 65–82 min
    What can we do there?

    Explain what visitors can do and recommend a place.

  7. 82–108 min
    Project workshop

    Write your route and speaking script; ask for teacher feedback.

  8. 108–117 min
    Mini-rehearsal & peer feedback

    Test your tour and help a partner improve.

  9. 117–120 min
    Exit ticket

    Choose one thing to improve before next week.

✓By the end: A planned map route, a short speaking script and peer feedback.

03 FRIDAY · 27 NOVEMBER · 120 MINUTESGuide your visitorRoute check · final tour · reflection ⌄

Today's goal Deliver your guided tour with confidence, evaluate your progress, and celebrate what you can now do.

  1. 0–10 min
    Welcome and warm-up

    Recall useful expressions and the success criteria.

  2. 10–25 min
    Final preparation

    Rehearse and make your last improvements.

  3. 25–35 min
    Route-check challenge

    Check that another person can follow your directions.

  4. 35–45 min
    Break

    10-minute break.

  5. 45–100 min
    Final presentations

    Present your three-stop tour with your partner.

  6. 100–112 min
    Self-assessment

    Recognise what you have achieved.

  7. 112–120 min
    Reflection & feedback

    Name one improvement and one future goal.

✓By the end: A partner presentation and a short self-assessment.

Words to help you start

⭐ RECOMMEND

“You can visit … I recommend … because …”
